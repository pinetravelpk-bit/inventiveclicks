Privacy Policy

A clear explanation of the information handled by this website.

Project briefs and candidate profiles

The forms can include your name, email, phone number, WhatsApp number, contact preference and project details. Saving a brief generates a file on your device and does not send it to Inventive Clicks or store it in an application database. Where WhatsApp sharing is available, you can review the message before opening WhatsApp and decide whether to send it. Avoid including confidential or sensitive information.

Hosting and network requests

Your browser requests website files from the hosting provider. The provider may handle technical information such as IP addresses and request logs under its own policies. Fonts are requested from Google Fonts; your browser may use system fonts if that service is unavailable.

Cookies and tracking

This theme does not include advertising pixels, analytics scripts or application cookies. Your hosting provider or a service used to access the private preview may apply its own authentication or technical controls.

External links and downloaded files

Downloaded briefs are stored on your device according to your browser settings. You decide whether to share them. External websites and services apply their own information-handling practices.

Future changes

This page should be reviewed when email delivery, analytics, a content system or other data-handling services are connected. The description above applies to the functionality currently included in this website.
