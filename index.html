What does Inventive Clicks do?+

Inventive Clicks provides digital marketing, SEO, PPC, social media, e-commerce, web development, branding, influencer marketing, content creation and remote staffing.

Yes. We can scope a project across disciplines, such as a new website with content and search planning. The proposal sets out the deliverables, responsibilities and review stages.

What should I share to get started?+

Tell us what your business does, who you need to reach, what needs to improve and any deadline. A link to your current website or examples of work you like will also help.
