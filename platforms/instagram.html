◈ BEFORE WE START

The content starts with your business.

Share your profiles, audience, available content and the action you want people to take. Identify who can approve factual claims and respond to customer questions.

Filming, creator fees and community coverage are specified in the proposal. Reach and follower growth are not guaranteed.

Official Instagram information

Scope and reporting

Organic work and paid campaigns can be commissioned separately or together. Your proposal identifies the outputs, review schedule, responsibilities and fees.

Advertising spend, platform subscriptions and commissioned production are separate unless the proposal explicitly includes them. Reporting distinguishes campaign-attributed activity from total business results.

◈ INSTAGRAM QUESTIONS

Plan with the right expectations.

Can one video be reused everywhere?+

Source footage can be adapted, but hooks, framing, captions and the intended action should fit each placement. Reuse also depends on the rights attached to the footage.

Can you manage both paid and organic marketing?+

Yes. We can coordinate both within one scope or focus on the work you need first. We keep the activities and reporting distinct so you can understand what each contributes.

Can you guarantee results?+

No. Competition, account eligibility, product or service quality, budget and customer experience affect outcomes. We agree useful measures and report limitations clearly.
