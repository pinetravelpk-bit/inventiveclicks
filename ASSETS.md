## Generation brief

1. Cinematic left-facing woman, neon cyan, violet, magenta and orange light across face and flowing hair; dark navy background, dark space on left; standalone artwork without UI or text.
2. Twelve glossy bevelled three-dimensional icons, consistent cyan/violet/pink illumination and a dark backdrop, equal 4 × 3 grid: megaphone, magnifying glass, bullseye, social symbols; shopping bag, code brackets, palette, person with heart; video tile, group of people, glass cube branded Inventive Clicks, metallic paper airplane.
3. Warm creative-agency meeting room, walnut table, black chairs, plants and dark walls with GOOD IDEAS GREAT RESULTS and Inventive Clicks signs.
4. Three square portfolio scenes: ecommerce smartphone/cosmetics on purple, website laptop/plant on teal, analytics laptop on blue. No marketing claims baked into the images.

The original reference image is retained for the existing logo and testimonial avatar. Generated images are illustrative; no claim is made that they depict actual client work, employees or office premises. Client marks and supplied reference content remain subject to their owners' rights.
