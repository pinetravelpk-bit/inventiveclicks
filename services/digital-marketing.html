◈ COMMON QUESTIONS

Your questions,
answered.

What affects the scope of a marketing plan?+

The number of markets, channels and offers matters, but so does the work already in place. A business with a usable website and clear positioning needs a different plan from one that still needs its offer, content and tracking prepared.

Can you work alongside our internal team?+

Yes. We can develop the strategy, support specific channels, or coordinate delivery with your existing specialists. Responsibilities are agreed at the start.

Do we need every marketing channel?+

No. We choose channels around audience fit, available resources and the action you want customers to take.

How is success measured?+

We agree on relevant measures such as qualified enquiries, purchases, conversion rate and acquisition cost. The exact measures depend on your business and tracking setup.
