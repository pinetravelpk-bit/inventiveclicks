◈ COMMON QUESTIONS

Your questions,
answered.

What should we agree before design begins?+

Specify the assets, intended uses, review rounds and final file formats. Choose one person to consolidate feedback. A logo project and a full identity system require different deliverables, and editable files or third-party licences should be discussed explicitly.

Can you keep our existing logo?+

Yes. A refresh can improve the surrounding visual system while preserving the logo and other established brand elements.

Are editable files included?+

Final formats and editable source files are specified in the proposal and handover checklist.

How do revisions work?+

We agree on review stages and revision rounds before work begins so feedback stays focused and the scope remains clear.
