◈ COMMON QUESTIONS

Your questions,
answered.

Can creator content be reused on our website or in ads?+

Only if the agreement permits those uses. Confirm the channels, duration, territories and any editing rights before production. Paid promotion through a creator account may require additional permissions. A fee for one published post should not be assumed to cover all future uses.

Are creator fees included?+

Creator fees, usage rights, production costs and management are itemized in the proposal.

Can we reuse influencer content in ads?+

Only when the agreement grants the required usage and advertising permissions. We define this before production.

Do you choose creators by follower count?+

Follower count is one input. Audience fit, content quality, engagement context and brand suitability also matter.
