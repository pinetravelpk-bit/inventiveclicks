◈ HOW WE WORK

From understanding
to action.

Define pages and functions

List the visitor journeys, content owners and required integrations. Distinguish essential launch features from later additions.

02

Use representative headings, images and form fields to assess the layout. Empty placeholders can hide problems that appear when content arrives.

03

Develop the agreed pages and test navigation, forms, keyboard access and responsive behaviour.

04

Launch with an owner

Confirm hosting, domain access and the editing workflow. Document who maintains dependencies, content and third-party services.

◈ COMMON QUESTIONS

Your questions,
answered.

What affects the website quote and launch date?+

Page count, custom functionality, integrations and content readiness all affect the work. Confirm whether copy, images, migration and ongoing maintenance are included. A finished-looking form also needs a delivery service and an agreed recipient before it can accept enquiries.

Can you build in Next.js?+

Yes. Next.js can be a suitable choice for a custom React website. We choose the architecture around editing, hosting and functional requirements.

Will the website work on mobile?+

Responsive behavior is part of the build. We review navigation, content and key interactions across representative screen sizes.

Can our team update the content?+

We agree on the editing workflow during discovery. A CMS can be included where it is needed; a code-based site requires a developer for content updates.
