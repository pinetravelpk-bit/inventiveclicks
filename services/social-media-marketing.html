◈ COMMON QUESTIONS

Your questions,
answered.

What can slow down a social content schedule?+

Missing product details, late approvals and a shortage of usable photos can delay production. Agree one approval contact and a place to collect assets. Customer-service replies and urgent complaints also need a clear escalation route rather than relying on a posting calendar.

Which platforms do you manage?+

The platform mix is agreed around your audience and needs. Scope can include Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and other relevant channels.

Will you need content from our team?+

Usually. Product details, real photographs, offers and subject-matter input help content reflect your actual business.

Is paid advertising included?+

Paid social can be coordinated with organic content, but media spend and campaign management are scoped explicitly.
