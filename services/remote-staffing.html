◈ REMOTE STAFFING

What does our
remote staffing service include?

A productive remote engagement needs more than a role title. We help define the responsibilities, required skills, tools and working arrangement so that the person joining your workflow has a clear starting point.

A good fit for

Businesses that need additional specialist capacity, recurring operational support or help defining a remote role before hiring.

◈ COMMON QUESTIONS

Your questions,
answered.

What should be agreed before a remote engagement starts?+

Confirm working hours, supervision, confidentiality, access, reporting and the scope of responsibility. A person joining your team needs different support from a provider delivering a defined service. Fees and availability depend on the role and arrangement; they are confirmed in the proposal.

Which roles can you help with?+

Scope can include marketing, SEO, design, development, customer support and business operations. Availability and suitability are confirmed for each request.

Can the team work in our time zone?+

Required working hours and overlap are discussed before candidate selection and included in the agreed arrangement.

Is this a managed service or staff augmentation?+

The engagement can be scoped around additional team capacity or defined managed deliverables. Ownership, supervision and responsibilities are clarified in the proposal.
