◈ COMMON QUESTIONS

Your questions,
answered.

Are renders a substitute for accurate product photography?+

Renders can illustrate a design, including products not yet manufactured. They should be labelled where needed and should not imply unverified product features or finished conditions.

Who is this service suitable for?+

Businesses that need visualisation and can supply dimensions, references or suitable source models.

How are fees and delivery dates agreed?+

We review the required outputs, available inputs and dependencies before proposing a scope. Fees and dates are confirmed in the proposal; third-party costs are identified separately.
