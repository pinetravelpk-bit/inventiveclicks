What your landing page needs before the design begins.

A practical content checklist for a page that explains your offer and makes the next step easy.

Inventive Clicks Editorial · 4 min read
Ideas that move
your business forward.
01

A headline that explains the offer

A visitor should be able to understand what the page is about without decoding a slogan. Pair a clear headline with a short explanation of who the offer is for and what problem it addresses.

02

Details that support a decision

List the information someone needs before contacting you or buying. Depending on the offer, that may include scope, process, service area, eligibility, delivery expectations or product specifications. Give essential details a clear place in the layout.

03

Evidence you can stand behind

Use relevant work, accurate customer feedback and specific information about your approach. Keep permissions and supporting material for testimonials and claims. Where you do not yet have proof, explain your process instead of filling the space with invented numbers.

04

A focused action

Choose a primary action and make its purpose clear. If a form is involved, ask only for what is useful at that point. Explain what happens after submission and ensure someone owns the follow-up.

05

A review on a small screen

Read the content in the order a mobile visitor will encounter it. Check that the main action remains easy to find, the headings are understandable and key information is not hidden in an image. Design can make good content easier to use; it cannot supply missing facts.
